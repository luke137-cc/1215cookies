About 12'15

12’15 Cookies is a small cookie business founded by Liam Rayala, based in Manila, Philippines.

We specialize in freshly baked homemade cookies made with quality ingredients and a passion for baking.

· What started as a simple love for baking grew into a small business dedicated to making every bite worth coming back for. ·

How to Order

1

Browse and Select Your Treats

Explore our menu of freshly baked cookies. Select your favorite flavors and quantities, then click Add to Cart.

2

Review Your Cart

Click the cart icon to check your order details such as the quantity of cookies before you proceed to Checkout.

3

Fill Up the Necessary Details

Enter your name, contact number and preferred fulfillment method. Review your order summary and click Copy Order once you're content with your order.

4

Send Your Order to our FB Page

Upon clicking Order Here , you will be redirected to our FB Page. Send the text you copied from the checkout section!

5

Pay 50% of the Payment & Receive a Confirmation Message

We require our customers to pay at least 50% of the total order price . Once we confirm your payment, we'll send a confirmation and process your order!


Updates will be sent in a timely manner. Thank you for ordering!
